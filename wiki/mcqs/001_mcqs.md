## q-001-3 · Best of five

A. The outcomes and trade-offs used to frame each person's decision
B. The completeness of the review's bibliographic search
C. The reproducibility of the trial's event-rate measurements
D. The conventional ranking of the treatment study design
E. The statistical precision of the pooled treatment effect

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Best response: A. The outcomes and trade-offs used to frame each person's decision

The same evidence can inform different decisions when valued outcomes and burdens differ.

**A.** The same evidence can inform different decisions when valued outcomes and burdens differ. (PDF 31)

**B.** Search completeness informs the evidence base, not which trade-off each person accepts. (PDF 31)

**C.** Measurement reliability affects trust in the estimates, not the patients' differing priorities. (PDF 31)

**D.** Design ranking cannot supply an individual's values. (PDF 31)

B. Whether recipients changed the relevant clinical behaviour
