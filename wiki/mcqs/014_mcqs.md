## q-014-1 · Best of five

After a care pathway is introduced, length of stay falls. During the same period the hospital also opens a discharge lounge and changes senior staffing. Which claim is best supported by an uncontrolled before-and-after comparison?

A. The pathway caused the whole reduction because it preceded measurement
B. The improvement occurred alongside several changes; attribution to the pathway is uncertain
C. The reduction is a measurement artefact because services changed
D. The pathway had no effect because another change occurred
E. The discharge lounge caused the whole reduction because it is a simpler explanation

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Best response: B. The improvement occurred alongside several changes; attribution to the pathway is uncertain

The observed association needs alternative explanations considered; concurrent changes weaken a single-cause claim.

**A.** Temporal order is necessary for causation but does not exclude concurrent causes. (PDF 234)

**B.** The observed association needs alternative explanations considered; concurrent changes weaken a single-cause claim. (PDF 234)

**C.** System changes create attribution uncertainty, not automatic evidence of artefactual measurement. (PDF 234)

**D.** Confounding does not establish a zero pathway effect. (PDF 234)

**E.** A plausible alternative is not proof that it explains the complete change. (PDF 234)

## q-014-5 · Data interpretation

A local project reports a shorter stay after implementation and recommends that every hospital copy the pathway unchanged. Which additional evidence most directly strengthens the transferable lesson?

A. A second local analysis restricted to staff who supported the pathway
B. A precise explanation of the observed outcome difference on the same local data
C. An endorsement of the pathway by the original project steering group
D. A count of implementation meetings held before the pathway started
E. A reasoned account of the mechanism, context and conditions for adaptation

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Best response: E. A reasoned account of the mechanism, context and conditions for adaptation

Transfer requires explaining why and under what conditions the change could work elsewhere.

**A.** Selecting supportive staff can add bias and still does not establish conditions for transfer. (PDF 236)

**B.** Precision of the local result does not establish why it would transfer to other settings. (PDF 236)

**C.** Endorsement is not evidence about mechanisms or the settings in which the pathway may work. (PDF 236)

**D.** Activity counts do not show how implementation conditions connect to outcomes. (PDF 236)

**E.** Transfer requires explaining why and under what conditions the change could work elsewhere. (PDF 236)
