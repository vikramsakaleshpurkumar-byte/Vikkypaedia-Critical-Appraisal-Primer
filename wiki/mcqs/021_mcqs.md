## q-021-1 · Best of five

## q-021-2 · Best of five

A team uses a systematic-review checklist. The search is clear and the studies are eligible, but interventions and outcomes differ enough that a common summary may be misleading. Which checklist response is most appropriate?

A. Replace each intervention name with a broad class label before pooling
B. Pool only the studies with individually significant effects
C. Use the largest effect as the summary for all interventions
D. Record that reasonable identification does not establish reasonable pooling
E. Mark the review complete because every eligible paper was retrieved

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Best response: D. Record that reasonable identification does not establish reasonable pooling

Selection and synthesis are separate judgements; a rigorous review can decide not to calculate one pooled effect.

**A.** Using a common label does not make differing interventions or outcomes sufficiently comparable for one pooled estimate. (PDF 328)

**B.** Selecting only statistically significant results can bias the pooled estimate and does not resolve clinical incompatibility. (PDF 328)

**C.** An extreme selected effect does not represent the body of evidence. (PDF 328)

**D.** Selection and synthesis are separate judgements; a rigorous review can decide not to calculate one pooled effect. (PDF 328)

**E.** Retrieval alone does not justify its numerical synthesis. (PDF 328)

## q-021-5 · Data interpretation

A learner completes a checklist before deciding what the patient's priority problem is. Every methods box is marked satisfactory. Which missing action should take priority before applying the study?

A. Choose the outcome with the lowest reported p-value

C. Check whether a second study reports a more precise estimate of the same outcome
D. Clarify the person's decision and whether the study addresses it

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

A technically satisfactory paper can answer a different question from the one this person needs resolved.
