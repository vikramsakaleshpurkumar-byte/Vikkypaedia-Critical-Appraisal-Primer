## q-018-1 · Best of five

A feeding programme uses nutritionally balanced meals but shows no growth effect. Observation finds that many children reject the unfamiliar food. Which inference best combines the outcome and process evidence?

A. The growth outcome should be replaced retrospectively by meal delivery

C. Balanced meals cannot improve growth in this age group
D. The observations should be excluded because they are non-randomised
E. Meal rejection proves that a culturally adapted programme would be effective

Best response: B. The intended nutritional exposure may not have occurred adequately

A plausible link in the mechanism failed; this limits what the negative result says about consuming an adequate supplement.

## q-018-5 · Data interpretation

A trial randomises advice to adopt a behaviour. Uptake is 25%, and the intention-to-advise comparison shows no clear benefit. Which claim is closest to what was tested?

A. The trial estimates the effect of the behaviour without dilution
B. The behaviour would benefit exactly the 25% who adopted it
C. The result demonstrates that advice cannot change behaviour
D. Offering that advice under those conditions showed no clear benefit
E. Performing the behaviour consistently has been shown ineffective

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Best response: D. Offering that advice under those conditions showed no clear benefit

The assigned intervention was advice; low uptake limits an inference about consistently performing the behaviour.

**A.** Assigned advice and actual behaviour are not identical exposures here. (PDF 294)

**B.** Uptake does not identify a guaranteed treatment response. (PDF 294)

**C.** Some uptake occurred, and the trial concerns the specified advice strategy and conditions. (PDF 294)

**D.** The assigned intervention was advice; low uptake limits an inference about consistently performing the behaviour. (PDF 294)
