## q-019-1 · Best of five

A consensus panel addresses treatment burden but includes only researchers who developed the intervention. The authors report a high agreement percentage. Which issue most directly limits interpretation of the agreement's scope?

A. Whether relevant clinical and lived-experience perspectives were represented
B. Whether the final threshold is stricter than thresholds in previous panels
C. Whether the panel met in person rather than online
D. Whether the final statements are more concise than the initial statements

## q-019-5 · Data interpretation

Two Delphi rounds both report 75% agreement. The identities of agreeing and disagreeing participants change markedly between rounds. What should be checked before calling the responses stable?

A. Whether the chosen threshold was below 75%
B. Whether the same percentage was reported before and after rounding
C. Only whether the mean agreement percentage remained 75%
D. Whether agreement was calculated after excluding the most variable respondents
E. Participant-level response changes and the pattern of persistent disagreement

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Best response: E. Participant-level response changes and the pattern of persistent disagreement

Equal aggregate percentages can conceal substantial movement in individual views.

**A.** Meeting a threshold concerns consensus status, not necessarily stability. (PDF 308)

**C.** The mean alone does not establish stability of responses. (PDF 308)

**D.** Excluding variable respondents could manufacture stability rather than evaluate it transparently. (PDF 308)
