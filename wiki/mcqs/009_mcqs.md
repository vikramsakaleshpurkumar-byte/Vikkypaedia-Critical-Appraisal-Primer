## q-009-5 · Data interpretation

In a forest plot of risk ratios, a small study has RR 0.60 with CI 0.20–1.80; a larger study has RR 0.85 with CI 0.76–0.95. Which interpretation best uses both effect and uncertainty?

A. The smaller study has a larger point benefit but much less precise evidence
B. The small study should be excluded because its interval crosses 1
C. The larger study has no clinically relevant effect because its RR is nearer 1
D. The smaller study provides stronger evidence because 0.60 is further from 1
E. The studies necessarily conflict because their point estimates differ

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Best response: A. The smaller study has a larger point benefit but much less precise evidence

Its point estimate is more favourable, but its interval permits substantial benefit and harm relative to no effect at 1.

**A.** Its point estimate is more favourable, but its interval permits substantial benefit and harm relative to no effect at 1. (PDF 158)

**B.** Lack of individual significance is not an eligibility criterion. (PDF 158)

**D.** Distance of the point estimate does not by itself establish evidential strength. (PDF 158)
