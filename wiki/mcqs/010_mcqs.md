## q-010-1 · Best of five

A guideline panel has strong subject expertise but no methods specialist or patient representative. Its recommendations concern burdensome long-term treatment. Which addition most directly addresses the missing perspectives?

A. Independent evidence-methods input and relevant lived-experience input
B. Two additional senior clinicians who developed the treatment
C. A copy editor and a graphic designer before publication
D. A larger panel drawn from the same specialist society

## q-010-5 · Data interpretation

A recommendation offers a small average benefit at the cost of frequent travel. The patient values independence but cannot reliably reach the clinic. Which information would most improve the guideline's practical application?

A. The number of hospitals that downloaded the guideline
B. Feasible alternatives and the patient's acceptable benefit-burden trade-off
C. A longer account of the disease's molecular pathway
D. A more precise ranking of the guideline's publication impact
E. The percentage of panel members agreeing with the recommendation

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

The decision requires a workable option aligned with the patient's circumstances and preferences.
