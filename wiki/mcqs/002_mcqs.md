## q-002-1 · Best of five

A librarian supports two projects about the same treatment. Project X needs an answer for tomorrow's consultation; project Y will claim a comprehensive map of the research. Both have found the same credible recent synthesis. Which difference in stopping rules is most defensible?

A. X may stop if the synthesis is statistically significant; Y should seek nonsignificant studies
B. X may stop after relevance checks; Y still needs a documented comprehensive search
C. Both should stop when a second search returns the same synthesis
D. Both should continue until each database returns exactly the same papers
E. X should retrieve every trial; Y may stop once the synthesis is methodologically sound

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Best response: B. X may stop after relevance checks; Y still needs a documented comprehensive search

Search purpose sets the completeness requirement; a useful answer and a comprehensive evidence map are different products.

## q-002-5 · Data interpretation

Search A retrieves 12 relevant papers from one database. Search B retrieves those 12 plus 3 relevant reports through citation chaining and a second database. A proposal states that A was comprehensive because its papers were high quality. Which observation directly challenges that claim?

A. Both searches found the same synthesis article
B. The 12 original papers had different study designs
C. Eligible relevant reports were missed by the single-database method
D. Search B retrieved more total records than search A
E. The three additional reports were less recently published

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Best response: C. Eligible relevant reports were missed by the single-database method

Comprehensiveness concerns coverage as well as the quality of retrieved records.

**A.** An overlapping result does not establish that their primary-study coverage is equivalent. (PDF 36)

**B.** Design diversity may be appropriate and does not itself show incomplete retrieval. (PDF 36)

**C.** Comprehensiveness concerns coverage as well as the quality of retrieved records. (PDF 36)

**D.** Total volume can increase without improving relevant coverage; the eligible additions are decisive. (PDF 36)
