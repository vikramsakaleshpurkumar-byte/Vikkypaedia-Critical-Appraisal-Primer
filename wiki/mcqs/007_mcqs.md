## q-007-1 · Best of five

C. Equipment plus calls versus published historical admission rates

## q-007-5 · Data interpretation

A complex intervention improves confidence but not disease severity. The prespecified outcomes included both. A report calls this a complete failure because severity was unchanged. Which interpretation best retains the outcome structure?

A. The confidence effect proves that delivery fidelity was adequate
B. Different outcomes show different effects and need separate interpretation
C. The severity result should be dropped because confidence improved
D. The confidence result establishes that disease severity will improve later
E. Both outcomes should be combined into an unplanned success score

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Best response: B. Different outcomes show different effects and need separate interpretation

A benefit in confidence is not a severity benefit, but neither should it be erased by an all-or-none label.

**A.** An outcome difference alone does not establish which components were delivered as intended. (PDF 123)

**B.** A benefit in confidence is not a severity benefit, but neither should it be erased by an all-or-none label. (PDF 123)

**C.** Prespecified outcomes should not be selected according to favourable findings. (PDF 123)
