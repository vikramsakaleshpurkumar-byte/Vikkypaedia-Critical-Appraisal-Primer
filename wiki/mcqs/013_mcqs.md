## q-013-1 · Best of five

A questionnaire gives very similar scores on repeat administration but its items focus on clinic convenience when the claimed construct is treatment effectiveness. What is the best interpretation of the measurement evidence?

A. Both properties are established if the mean score is high
B. Validity is established because a stable score is unlikely to be random
C. Neither property can be discussed until a randomised trial is completed

E. Repeatability is supported, while validity for the claimed construct remains doubtful

Consistent measurement of convenience does not establish measurement of treatment effectiveness.

## q-013-5 · Data interpretation

A survey receives 180 responses from 200 invitations in clinic A and 60 from 200 in clinic B. Most non-responders in B cannot use the online form. Which comparison is most defensible before pooling satisfaction scores?

A. Assess how access-related non-response could change the represented groups

C. Weight each observed score only by the number of invitation emails
D. Use only the clinic with the larger response rate to estimate both clinics
E. Treat the two response means as equally representative because both exceed 50 replies

<details><summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

Best response: A. Assess how access-related non-response could change the represented groups

Different response mechanisms can distort the comparison even when invitation counts match.
