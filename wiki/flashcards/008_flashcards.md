<details><summary>What is sensitivity?</summary>

The proportion of people with the target condition who test positive: true positives divided by all with the condition.

PDF p. 131
</details>

<details><summary>What is specificity?</summary>

The proportion without the target condition who test negative: true negatives divided by all without the condition.

PDF p. 131
</details>

<details><summary>What does positive predictive value ask?</summary>

Among people with a positive result, what proportion truly have the condition? It depends on the population's disease prevalence.

PDF p. 133
</details>

<details><summary>Why must the reference standard be independent of the new test?</summary>

Including the new test in the reference definition can make validation circular and bias accuracy estimates.

PDF p. 135
</details>

<details><summary>What is verification or work-up bias?</summary>

Accuracy is distorted when receipt of the reference standard depends on the index test result, such as verifying positives only.

PDF p. 136
</details>

<details><summary>How is a likelihood ratio used to update diagnostic probability?</summary>

Convert pre-test probability to odds, multiply by the appropriate likelihood ratio, then convert post-test odds to probability.
