<details><summary>How is control event rate calculated?</summary>

Control participants with the event divided by all control participants: a/(a+b).

PDF p. 335
</details>

<details><summary>How is experimental event rate calculated?</summary>

Experimental participants with the event divided by all experimental participants: c/(c+d).

PDF p. 335
</details>

<details><summary>How is relative risk calculated?</summary>

Experimental event rate divided by control event rate, provided control event rate is nonzero.

PDF p. 335
</details>

<details><summary>How is absolute risk reduction calculated for an undesirable event?</summary>

Control event rate minus experimental event rate; use proportions and preserve the outcome and time horizon.

PDF p. 335
</details>

<details><summary>How is relative risk reduction calculated?</summary>

Derived from the relative-risk formula: RRR = 1 − RR = (CER − EER)/CER, when CER is nonzero. This is an authored algebraic explanation of the source formulas.

PDF p. 335
</details>

<details><summary>How is NNT calculated for a positive absolute risk reduction?</summary>

The reciprocal of absolute risk reduction expressed as a proportion, for the specified outcome and follow-up period.

</details>
