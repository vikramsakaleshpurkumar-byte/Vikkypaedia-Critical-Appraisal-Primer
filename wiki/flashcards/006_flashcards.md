<details><summary>Why examine the comparator in a drug trial?</summary>

<details><summary>Why inspect patient selection in a treatment trial?</summary>

The benefit-harm balance may not transfer to patients with different characteristics or comorbidities.

</details>

<details><summary>What is a surrogate endpoint?</summary>

An intermediate measure used in place of a more directly patient-important outcome; its predictive validity needs evidence.

PDF p. 107
</details>

<details><summary>Why can a plausible surrogate mislead?</summary>

Changing a marker need not produce the expected benefit in clinical outcomes, particularly if other effects intervene.

PDF p. 110
</details>

<details><summary>Why can an efficacy RCT miss rare serious harms?</summary>

Trials powered for common efficacy outcomes are often too small to detect events occurring only once in many thousands.
