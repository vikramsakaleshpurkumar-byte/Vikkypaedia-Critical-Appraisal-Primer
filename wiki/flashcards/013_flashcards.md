<details><summary>How do validity and reliability differ?</summary>

Validity concerns measuring the intended construct; reliability concerns consistency of measurement.

</details>

<details><summary>What instructions help participants answer appropriately?</summary>

Explain purpose, confidentiality, voluntariness, contact details and how to complete each item.

</details>

<details><summary>Why can an opening-hours survey of current clinic attenders mislead?</summary>

People unable to attend at those hours are systematically missing from the sample.

</details>

<details><summary>Why pilot a questionnaire?</summary>

To identify problems in wording, layout and completion before the main survey and improve its practical usability.
