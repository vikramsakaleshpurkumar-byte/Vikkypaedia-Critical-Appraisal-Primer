Its content, the process used to develop it, and how it is presented to intended users.

<details><summary>What expertise should a guideline panel draw on?</summary>

Topic expertise, evidence-methods expertise and the experience of people affected by the condition.

</details>

<details><summary>How should a recommendation connect to evidence?</summary>

Through a transparent search and appraisal of relevant research, with uncertainty and evidence strength made clear.

</details>

<details><summary>Why assess patient acceptability and practical feasibility?</summary>

An apparently sound recommendation may be unaffordable, burdensome or unacceptable to the people expected to follow it.
