<details><summary>What is the Hawthorne effect relevant to quality improvement?</summary>

Performance can change because staff receive attention or know a change is being studied, not solely because of its intrinsic content.

</details>

Research claims need justified lessons transferable beyond the local site, with context and limitations explained.
