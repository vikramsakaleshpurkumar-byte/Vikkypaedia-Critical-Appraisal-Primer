## Editorial findings and corrections

The earlier bank relied too often on obviously irrelevant options and a broadly qualified correct answer. The revision uses appraisal problems with competing actions or interpretations, explicit assumptions and a defined lead-in. Misconceptions include confusing trial selection with allocation, documentation with delivered care, relative with absolute effects, precision with validity, reference existence with claim support and aggregate consensus with response stability.

Residual off-domain distractors in the first rewritten draft were removed during the editorial pass. Two assertion–reason pairs with potentially debatable explanatory links were rewritten. A likelihood-ratio distractor and explanation were corrected to the intended probability-addition error. Additional source links were added where the two statements draw on different topics.
