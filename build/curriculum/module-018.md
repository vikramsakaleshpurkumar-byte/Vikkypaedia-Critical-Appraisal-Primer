## Case stages and feedback

Reused stage 1: A feeding programme uses nutritionally balanced meals but shows no growth effect. Observation finds that many children reject the unfamiliar food. Which inference best combines the outcome and process evidence?

Feedback: A plausible link in the mechanism failed; this limits what the negative result says about consuming an adequate supplement. (PDF 288). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.

Reused stage 2: A trial suggests benefit but the proposed mechanism is uncertain. A laboratory study supports one causal pathway but cannot estimate clinical benefit. Which next synthesis is most appropriate?

Feedback: Each addresses different parts of the inference; their quality and fit should be examined together. (PDF 291). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.
