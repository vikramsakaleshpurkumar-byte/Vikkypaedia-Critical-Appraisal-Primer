## Case stages and feedback

Reused stage 1: A review team excludes trials after seeing that their effects run opposite to its expected result, although they meet the question and quality criteria. The team cites reduced heterogeneity as justification. Which principle should govern inclusion?

Feedback: Direction of a finding is not a defensible reason to remove an otherwise eligible study. (PDF 151). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.

Reused stage 2: A review assigns every trial a total quality score. Trial X scores well overall but has a serious allocation problem. The review treats it as low risk because its total exceeds the cut-off. Which concern most directly challenges this classification?

Feedback: A favourable sum does not demonstrate that a serious design flaw has been neutralised. (PDF 154). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.
