## Case stages and feedback

Reused stage 1: A questionnaire gives very similar scores on repeat administration but its items focus on clinic convenience when the claimed construct is treatment effectiveness. What is the best interpretation of the measurement evidence?

Feedback: Consistent measurement of convenience does not establish measurement of treatment effectiveness. (PDF 218). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.

Reused stage 2: A team adapts a previously validated questionnaire for a different language and patient group, changing several response categories. Which claim now needs particular justification?

Feedback: Prior validation is useful evidence, but substantive adaptations and a new population can change measurement behaviour. (PDF 217). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.
