## Case stages and feedback

Reused stage 1: A genome-wide study identifies a SNP associated with a disease. The SNP lies near a biologically plausible gene, but no functional work has tested the variant. Which next finding most directly strengthens a causal interpretation of this variant itself?

Feedback: Association and proximity do not establish which variant is causal; functional linkage tests a missing causal step. (PDF 243). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.

Reused stage 2: A discovery study reports a large variant effect just beyond its significance threshold. A well-designed replication estimates a smaller effect in the same direction. Which interpretation is most defensible before examining further evidence?

Feedback: The winner's curse can favour overestimated effects at initial detection; replication can improve estimation. (PDF 252). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.
