## Case stages and feedback

Reused stage 1: A trial estimates a risk ratio of 0.86 with a 95% confidence interval of 0.64–1.16. A reviewer calls it evidence of no benefit. What is the most defensible correction?

Feedback: The estimate remains imprecise; a nonsignificant result has not established no important benefit. (PDF 95). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.

Reused stage 2: A trial protocol prespecifies one primary comparison. The paper instead highlights a significant result found after exploring 18 subgroups, while the primary comparison is nonsignificant. Which additional information best addresses the credibility of the subgroup claim?

Feedback: Post-hoc searching raises multiplicity and selective interpretation concerns; prespecification and replication help assess credibility. (PDF 94). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.
