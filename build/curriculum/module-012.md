## Case stages and feedback

Reused stage 1: Researchers seek to explain why some patients find a service accessible and others avoid it. They deliberately recruit contrasting users and non-users, rather than a random sample of current attenders. Which appraisal criterion best fits this choice?

Feedback: Purposive selection can appropriately explore relevant contrasts; it is not intended to estimate a population average by itself. (PDF 205). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.

Reused stage 2: An interviewer is also a clinician in the service being studied. The report describes how this role affected recruitment, questioning and interpretation. What can a reader reasonably infer from this account?

Feedback: Reflexivity improves transparency; it does not claim the researcher had no influence. (PDF 206). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.
