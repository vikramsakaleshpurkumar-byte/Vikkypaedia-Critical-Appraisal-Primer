## Case stages and feedback

Reused stage 1: A consensus panel addresses treatment burden but includes only researchers who developed the intervention. The authors report a high agreement percentage. Which issue most directly limits interpretation of the agreement's scope?

Feedback: Agreement within a narrow panel does not establish agreement across omitted kinds of expertise. (PDF 306). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.

Reused stage 2: A group wants to explore expert views while reducing direct pressure from senior speakers. Which feature of a Delphi process most directly serves that purpose?

Feedback: Anonymity can reduce direct interpersonal pressure while iteration allows reconsideration. (PDF 303). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.
