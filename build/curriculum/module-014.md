## Case stages and feedback

Reused stage 1: After a care pathway is introduced, length of stay falls. During the same period the hospital also opens a discharge lounge and changes senior staffing. Which claim is best supported by an uncontrolled before-and-after comparison?

Feedback: The observed association needs alternative explanations considered; concurrent changes weaken a single-cause claim. (PDF 234). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.

Reused stage 2: A quality-improvement report gives outcome changes but little account of how the pathway was delivered. A second hospital wants to know whether a similar approach would work there. Which additional information would most directly improve that judgement?

Feedback: This connects the change process to context and helps another team judge what to reproduce or adapt. (PDF 233). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.
