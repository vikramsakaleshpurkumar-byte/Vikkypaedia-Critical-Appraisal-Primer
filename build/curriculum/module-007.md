## Case stages and feedback

Reused stage 1: An intervention arm receives home-monitoring equipment and weekly nurse calls. Controls receive neither. Hospital use is lower in the intervention arm. The proposed claim is that equipment alone reduces admissions. Which additional comparison most directly addresses that claim?

Feedback: Holding the co-intervention constant helps isolate the incremental contribution attributed to equipment. (PDF 122). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.

Reused stage 2: A multisite programme standardises its hypothesised active component while adapting appointment times and materials to local needs. Which development finding would most justify treating those features as adaptable rather than core?

Feedback: The core/adaptable distinction should be informed by development work and the programme's theory of action. (PDF 121). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.
