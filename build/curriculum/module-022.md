## Case stages and feedback

Reused stage 1: Over one year, 15 of 100 controls and 10 of 100 treated participants have an undesirable event. Which statement correctly compares absolute and relative effects?

Feedback: The risk difference is 0.15 − 0.10; relative risk is 0.10/0.15. (PDF 335). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.

Reused stage 2: A report compares recovery, a desirable outcome, in 60 of 100 treated people and 40 of 100 controls at three months. Which calculation gives the number needed to treat for one additional recovery over that period?

Feedback: For a desirable event, use the absolute benefit increase rather than the undesirable-event sign convention. (PDF 335). Response required before feedback; Continue before next stage.
