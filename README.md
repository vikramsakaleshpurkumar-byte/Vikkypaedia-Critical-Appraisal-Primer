# Vikkypaedia Course on How to Read a Paper

Open **index.html** in a modern browser, with all folders together. Everything runs locally; no account, internet connection or server is required. For another computer, extract the ZIP first and then open index.html. Do not open the HTML from inside the ZIP.

## Choose a route

- New to appraisal: start at units 1–5, use Essentials, then move to a study design relevant to you.
- Practising clinician or journal club: choose a relevant unit, use Application and staged decision practice, then check the source and discuss uncertainty.
- Research learner or educator: use Deep Dive, the original pages, outcome maps and full-source Markdown. Deep Dive is introductory advanced appraisal, not specialist methods training.

The Learn & read tab contains guided learning plus the full reader. Start here adds a new course dashboard, and AI Reading Lab adds a separate current-evidence pathway. The seven original study modes remain: reader, concept atlas, topic explorer, appraisal toolkit, flashcards, quiz studio and visual spotter. Source chapter names are retained for provenance; The course is named Vikkypaedia Course on How to Read a Paper, as requested.

## What is delivered

20 chapters plus 2 appendices; PDF 20–335 (printed 1–316), with complete original PDF preserving front matter and index. 132 authored cards; 110 distinct authored MCQs (six cards and five questions per unit, including each appendix). 132 guided explanation/recall units; 22 two-stage decision exercises reusing 44 of the MCQs, plus one original progressive three-stage capstone with three additional decisions (not counted in the 110-MCQ bank). No automated passage-recognition items. Three depth choices plus All. Source links include verified printed and PDF page mapping.

Full-page images preserve every in-scope page. Automatic text, tables and captions can have reading-order or encoding limitations. Original pages and PDF are authoritative. The source appendix's formulas are taught using authored fictional numbers. No current guideline update is asserted.

## Study and progress

Cards: Space to flip, arrow keys to move, 1 for review, 2 for mastered. Anki: import anki_course.tsv as Front / Back / Tags, separator tab, HTML allowed. The TSV includes 132 notes plus importer directives.

Card status, guided completion and saved reflections use browser-local storage. They do not sync between devices and may be cleared by the browser. The outcome view can export a JSON learning record. Recall text is temporary until a reflection is explicitly saved. A completed unit records the learner's action; it is not a competence certificate.

Quizzes are blind to chapter labels before grading and reveal source-linked correct and distractor explanations after answering. Repeat attempts and the staged exercises reuse questions, so improvement does not establish independent learning gains. Review cards tomorrow and next week if helpful; no scheduler is included.
